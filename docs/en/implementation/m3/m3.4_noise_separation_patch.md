# M3.4 — Noise Separation Patch

> English semantic mirror of the detailed Russian patch specification.

## Purpose

Estimate whether an observed signal is distinguishable from an explicit noise baseline.

## Preserved decisions

- Compare signal structure against a versioned baseline rather than using raw volume alone.
- Report unavailable, stale, or domain-mismatched baselines.
- Preserve reasons, confidence, missing inputs, and partial status.
- Avoid treating virality or message count as objective support.
- Keep threshold and percentile assumptions visible and testable.

## Boundary

Noise separation depends on the quality and relevance of the baseline. A poor baseline can make ordinary variation appear exceptional.
