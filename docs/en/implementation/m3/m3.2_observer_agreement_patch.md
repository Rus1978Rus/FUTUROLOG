# M3.2 — Observer Agreement Patch

> English semantic mirror of the detailed Russian patch specification.

## Purpose

Estimate whether sufficiently independent observers or analytical channels reach compatible conclusions about the same signal.

## Preserved decisions

- Separate observer identity, independence, result, confidence, and missing evidence.
- Do not treat duplicate pipelines or copied reports as independent agreement.
- Represent disagreement explicitly rather than averaging it away.
- Return component value, confidence, reasons, missing inputs, and partial status.
- Preserve inactive-layer fallback and active-weight normalization.

## Boundary

Agreement is evidence of consistency, not proof of truth. Correlated observers can agree for the same wrong reason.
