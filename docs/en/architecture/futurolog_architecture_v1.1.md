# FUTUROLOG Architecture v1.1

> **English semantic mirror.** This edition preserves the decisions, interfaces, constraints, status boundaries, and implementation intent of the Russian source. It is meaning-oriented rather than sentence-by-sentence. Canonical identifiers, formulas, API names, reason codes, and compatibility requirements remain unchanged.

## Purpose

Defines the current architectural target for detecting persistent emerging processes and estimating both risk intensity and evidential objectivity.

## Preserved decisions

- The hot path produces fast universal risk estimates.
- The audit path performs slower evidence enrichment and verification.
- Universal Risk Score and Objective Risk Score are separate outputs.
- Trust adjustment is applied once, while objective activation is conditional.
- Every prediction must remain explainable through reasons, evidence, provenance, and audit records.
- Compensation preserves error history and supports correction rather than erasing failed forecasts.
- The system supports domain adapters rather than embedding one marketplace-specific ontology.

## Boundaries and verification status

- The architecture is a research and engineering specification, not a validated forecasting product.
- Prediction quality, thresholds, and weights require domain datasets and prospective evaluation.

## Implementation meaning

Potential applications include OSINT, geopolitical monitoring, cyber defence, supply-chain risk, scientific trend detection, epidemiological surveillance, manufacturing diagnostics, and market monitoring. In every domain the system detects sustained structured change; it does not guarantee future outcomes.

## Source relationship

The Russian source remains the detailed normative working artifact. This English mirror is the public semantic counterpart and must be updated whenever a decision, invariant, interface, formula, or status changes.
